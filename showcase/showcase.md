# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. A field that refuses to stand still. Carom is a fast,
top-down duel where a clean return is just the beginning. Take on the machine
in Solo or pull up a second player for a shared-keyboard Versus match.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your rhythm. Then each paddle hit
adds speed, the gaps get smaller, and a comfortable rally becomes a test of
nerve. Reach eleven with a two-point lead to take the match; at ten-all,
every return matters.

Slide into the ball at contact and put a curve into its flight. Catch it near
the end of your paddle for a sharp angle, or meet it in the middle for a direct
shot. A mint trail traces the bend as your spin gradually fades.

The two amber obstacles sway and rotate between you and the goal. Their faces
can send a shot into a wall, back toward you, or through a gap your opponent
never saw coming. Read the moving geometry, trust the bank, and put a little
spin on it.
